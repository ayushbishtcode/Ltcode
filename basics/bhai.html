Good to Know
💡 Quick Tips for Bhai
🛶

Book Houseboat Early

Premium AC houseboats fill up fast in April. Book at least 3 weeks in advance. Must be AC — April is hot.

🌿

Eravikulam Entry

Book Eravikulam National Park tickets online in advance — they limit daily visitors and it sells out.

🐘

Periyar Safari Timing

Only the 6 AM slot gives good wildlife sightings. The afternoon slot is mostly just water views.

🌡️

April Weather

Kochi/Alappuzha/Varkala = 33–36°C, pack light cotton. Munnar/Thekkady = 22–26°C, pleasant and cool.

🕍

Padmanabhaswamy Temple

Mandatory dress code — men in dhoti, women in saree or salwar. No entry in western clothes.

💆

Couples Ayurvedic Spa

Do this in Kovalam — Kerala Ayurveda is world-class. Book a 90-min couples package at the resort.
